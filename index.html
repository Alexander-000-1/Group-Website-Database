Water Aid Overview:

WaterAid is an international charity dedicated to improving access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene worldwide. This system has been designed to support WaterAid staff in monitoring and recording the progress of water projects in communities that lack reliable access to safe water. Staff can track installation status, update project information, and maintain accurate reports of ongoing work.

Our Real World Challenge:

The challenge WaterAid faces is improving access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene across the globe. The regions requiring the most urgent aid include parts of Sub-Saharan Africa and South/Southeast Asia, particularly communities affected by climate change, droughts, and flooding. Effective data collection is essential to identify communities most in need, monitor project progress, and track budget requirements and donor contributions. Without a structured data system, prioritising resources and measuring long-term impact becomes extremely difficult.

Our SDG Alignment:

WaterAid directly aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 6 — to ensure the availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all. This system supports that goal by enabling effective project monitoring and informed decision making across the organisation.

About This Website:

This website serves as a web-based monitoring and reporting platform built to support the work of WaterAid staff across the globe. The system brings together key project data in one place, making it easier to track, manage and report on the progress of water projects in communities that need it most.

What the system does:

The platform allows users to record and manage details of WaterAid projects, monitor the installation and construction progress of water points, store and access community population and water consumption data, track donor contributions and funding progress, and generate reports to support informed decision making.

Who is it for?

Project Staff can use the system to oversee current builds, monitor donation levels and review community data at a glance. Field Workers can submit maintenance reports directly from their sites, including updating the operational status of water pumps. Donors can access summary reports and impact data, giving them a clear picture of how their contributions are making a difference in communities around the world.

About us

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 6: ensuring access to clean and safe drinking water for communities around the world.

Our mission is to support sustainable water solutions in underserved regions through infrastructure development, education, and community partnerships. By working closely with local leaders and volunteers, we aim to create long-lasting impact, improve public health, and empower communities to manage their own water resources. We believe that access to clean water is a basic human right, and we are committed to making it a reality for everyone.
